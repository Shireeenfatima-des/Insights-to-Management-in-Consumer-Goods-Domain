# Insights-to-Management-in-Consumer-Goods-Domain
## Overview of the Project
This project focuses on analyzing computer hardware sales data for Atliq Hardware, a leading producer in India and other countries.  The primary objective is to provide data-driven insights to improve decision-making within the company. The analysis involves using SQL to address ad-hoc requests related to markets, product performance, sales, and customer behavior.
## Ad-Hoc Requests: 
1.  Provide the list of markets in which customer  "Atliq  Exclusive"  operates its business in the  APAC  region.

2.  What is the percentage of unique product increase in 2021 vs. 2020? The final output contains these fields,unique_products_2020, unique_products_2021, percentage_chg 
  
3.  Provide a report with all the unique product counts for each  segment  and sort them in descending order of product counts. The final output contains fields:segment, product_count 
  
4.  Follow-up: Which segment had the most increase in unique products in 2021 vs 2020? The final output contains these fields, segment product_count_2020, product_count_2021, difference 

5.  Get the products that have the highest and lowest manufacturing costs. The final output should contain these fields: product_code, product, manufacturing_cost 
 
6.  Generate a report which contains the top 5 customers who received an average high  pre_invoice_discount_pct  for the  fiscal  year 2021  and in the Indian  market. The final output contains these fields, customer_code, customer 
average_discount_percentage

7.  Get the complete report of the Gross sales amount for the customer  “Atliq Exclusive”  for each month . This analysis helps to  get an idea of low and high-performing months and take strategic decisions. The final report contains these columns: Month ,Year ,Gross sales Amount

8.  In which quarter of 2020, got the maximum total_sold_quantity? The final output contains these fields sorted by the total_sold_quantity, Quarter, total_sold_quantity

9.  Which channel helped to bring more gross sales in the fiscal year 2021 and the percentage of contribution?  The final output  contains these fields, channel, gross_sales_mln 
prcentage

### Here's a quantitative description of the project, focusing on the numerical data and results:

This project analyzes Atliq Hardware's data, focusing on key quantitative metrics. It reveals a 36.33% increase in unique products from 2020 to 2021 (from 245 to 334 products). The project identifies product counts for each segment, with "Notebook" having the highest count at 129 and "Networking" the lowest at 9.   

In terms of sales, the analysis shows monthly gross sales for 'Atliq Exclusive', ranging from 2.8 million in the lowest month to 32.2 million in the highest.  Channel analysis for fiscal year 2021 indicates Flipkart contributed the most to gross sales with 30.83%, while Amazon contributed 29.33%.   

The project also provides manufacturing cost analysis, identifying the highest cost product at $240.54 and the lowest at $0.89.
